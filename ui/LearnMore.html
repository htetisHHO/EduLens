UNDERSTAND EVERY RESULT
Credibility Assessment
A credibility assessment estimates how trustworthy the information appears based on available evidence. High Evidence means reliable sources support the claim; Low Evidence means there isn’t enough trustworthy information to support it. Remember: this is an assessment, not absolute proof.
AI Explanation
Our AI explains why a claim received its credibility assessment in clear, simple language.
Educational Tips
Learn fact-checking habits you can use on your own: check the author, compare multiple reliable sources, look for supporting evidence, and verify publication dates before trusting or sharing information.
Trusted Sources
Review the trusted sources used in the analysis. We prioritize reliable organizations, educational institutions, government agencies, and reputable publications so you can examine the evidence yourself instead of relying only on AI.
Recent Analyses
Your recent analyses are saved in one place. Revisit previous checks, compare results, review the evidence, and keep track of the information you’ve verified.
Verification Streak
Build your verification streak by checking information before believing or sharing it online. Every verification strengthens your critical thinking and media literacy skills, one fact at a time.
